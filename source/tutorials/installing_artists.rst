======================================
Installing Art/Music Creation Programs 
======================================

This is a guide on how to install our club's recommended software for 3D modeling, 2D art, pixel art, and music/sound design.

Prerequisites
~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Before installing your tools, decide what area of game creation you want to focus on:

* **3D Art:** Blender
* **2D Concept & Texture Art:** Krita
* **2D Sprite & Pixel Art:** LibreSprite
* **Music & Sound Effects (SFX):** BandLab (Browser-based) or LMMS (Offline)

.. note::
   You do not need to install every single program! Download the tools that match your specific role on the team.

3D Modeling: Blender
--------------------

Blender is our primary 3D software for modeling, rigging, texturing, and animating game assets.

1. Go to the `Official Blender Download Page <https://www.blender.org/download/>`_.
2. Click the download button for your operating system (**Windows**, **macOS**, or **Linux**).
3. Run the setup installer and follow the wizard steps.
4. Open Blender once installation completes. On the initial splash screen, keep the default shortcut settings and click **Save New Settings**.

2D Concept Art & Texturing: Krita
---------------------------------

Krita is a powerful, free digital painting program used for drawing concept art, UI elements, and textures.

1. Go to the `Official Krita Download Page <https://krita.org/en/download/krita-desktop/>`_.
2. Download the installer for your operating system:
   
   * **Windows:** Download the 64-bit Installer executable.
   * **macOS:** Download the Disk Image (DMG) file.
   * **Linux:** Download the official AppImage file.

3. Run the installer and open Krita to confirm it launches properly.

2D Pixel Art & Animation: LibreSprite
-------------------------------------

LibreSprite is an open-source tool designed specifically for creating 2D pixel art and animated sprite sheets.

1. Go to the `Official LibreSprite Release Page <https://libresprite.github.iowebsite>`_.
2. Download the version corresponding to your operating system.
3. Extract or install the application and launch it to start creating pixel assets.

Music & Sound Design: BandLab or LMMS
-------------------------------------

We offer two options for music composition and sound effect creation depending on whether you prefer online collaboration or an offline workflow.

Option A: BandLab (Web-Based / Recommended)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

BandLab runs entirely in your web browser with zero installation required, making it easy to collaborate with teammates remotely.

1. Go to `BandLab <https://www.bandlab.com/>`_ and create a free account.
2. Verify your email address.
3. Click **Create** in the top navigation bar to open the Mix Editor directly in your browser.

Option B: LMMS (Offline DAW)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

If you prefer working offline or need a local Digital Audio Workstation for retro synth music and chiptunes:

1. Go to the `Official LMMS Download Page <https://lmms.io/download/>`_.
2. Select your platform (**Windows**, **macOS**, or **Linux**) and download the installer.
3. Run the installer and launch LMMS.

You have now installed your Art & Audio tools! Make your way over to the Art & Audio Overview to learn how to prepare assets for game engine integration!
